Spektra RMS Grain

An OpenFX grain node for DaVinci Resolve (macOS, Apple Silicon and Intel).

It installs SpektraRMSGrain.ofx.bundle into /Library/OFX/Plugins. Quit DaVinci Resolve before installing; after installing, find it under Effects › OpenFX › VividColour › Spektra RMS Grain.

Film modeling inspired by spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato. Film profile data CC BY-SA 4.0. Plugin licensed GPL-3.0.
