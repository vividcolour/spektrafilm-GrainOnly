Spektra RMS Grain
Film grain OFX plugin for DaVinci Resolve (macOS)


INSTALL

1. Quit DaVinci Resolve.
2. Run "Install Spektra RMS Grain.pkg". It installs to /Library/OFX/Plugins.
3. In Resolve's Color page, find it under OpenFX > VividColour >
   Spektra RMS Grain. Put it on its own node near the end of the tree,
   after your look.

The installer isn't signed. If macOS won't open it, right-click the pkg and
choose Open.


UNINSTALL

Run "Uninstall.command" (it asks for your password), or delete
/Library/OFX/Plugins/SpektraRMSGrain.ofx.bundle.


CONTROLS

Film Stock - 20 camera stocks from spektrafilm. Sets the curves and the RMS
  granularity.
Film Format - Super 8 up to IMAX 15/70. Smaller formats give bigger grain.
Custom RMS - turn on to type your own RMS value instead of the stock's.
Exposure Offset - where the image sits on the stock's curve. Overexposing
  gives finer grain, underexposing coarser.

Grain Amount - 1.0 is the stock's data sheet strength.
Grain Aspect Ratio - 1.0 is round. Above 1 stretches the grain horizontally,
  below 1 vertically. Overall strength stays the same.
Grain Size, Softness, Saturation (0 = mono), Animate, Seed.

Colour Space > Input - the colour space coming into the node. Defaults to
  DaVinci Wide Gamut / Intermediate.
Colour Space > Output - leave on Match Input and the image is untouched apart
  from the grain. Pick a space if you want the node to convert.

View - final image, or the grain on its own over mid grey.
